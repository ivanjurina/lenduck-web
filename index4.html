FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Lenduck free for businesses?

Yes, completely free. We're paid by lenders when they successfully provide financing through our platform.

How do you protect my data?

Your data is encrypted and stays anonymous. Lenders only see financial profiles—your identity is revealed only when you proceed with an offer.

Which accounting software do you support?

We support QuickBooks, Xero, Sage, FreshBooks, NetSuite, and 20+ others.

Does applying affect my credit score?

No. Getting matched and viewing offers doesn't affect your credit. A hard check only happens if you proceed with a lender.

What types of financing are available?

Business loans, lines of credit, invoice factoring, equipment financing, and more.

How do lenders benefit from Lenduck?

Lenders get pre-qualified leads with verified data, reducing acquisition costs. They only pay when they fund a deal.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Lenduck free for businesses?

Yes, completely free. We're paid by lenders when they successfully provide financing to businesses through our platform. You'll never pay us a cent.

How do you protect my data?

Your data is encrypted and stays completely anonymous. Lenders only see financial profiles - your company identity is only revealed when you choose to proceed with a specific offer.

Which accounting software do you support?

We support all major accounting platforms including QuickBooks, Xero, Sage, FreshBooks, NetSuite, and 20+ others. If you don't see yours, let us know.

Does applying affect my credit score?

No. Getting matched and viewing offers doesn't affect your credit. A hard credit check only happens if you choose to proceed with a specific lender.

What types of financing are available?

Business loans, lines of credit, invoice factoring, equipment financing, and more. Our lender network covers most SME financing needs.

How do lenders benefit from Lenduck?

Lenders get pre-qualified leads with verified financial data, dramatically reducing their acquisition costs and underwriting time. They only pay when they fund a deal.
